# Corkboard Community Guidelines — Enforcement Policy v1

Corkboard is a neighborhood app where verified local residents post recommendations, items
for sale, events, lost-and-found notices and local news. Reviewers apply this policy to
reported posts and choose exactly one action.

## Actions
- **ALLOW** — the post stays up unchanged.
- **LIMIT** — the post stays up but is hidden from the main feed and carries a notice.
  Use for content that is allowed but low quality, misleading, or likely to cause conflict.
- **REMOVE** — the post is taken down and the author is notified of the rule broken.
- **ESCALATE** — remove the post AND send it to the Safety team, which can contact police
  or emergency services. Use only when a real person may be in danger.

## 1. Harassment and bullying
Corkboard is for neighbors, and disagreement is allowed. Attacks on people are not.
- REMOVE: insults, slurs or mockery aimed at a specific identifiable person; repeated
  unwanted contact; encouraging others to target someone.
- ALLOW: criticism of a business's service, a public official's decisions, or an idea,
  even when harsh.
- ESCALATE: threats of violence against a person, including conditional threats.

## 2. Scams and fraud
- REMOVE: offers designed to take money or personal information under false pretenses
  (fake rentals, advance-fee requests, fake prizes, requests to pay with gift cards).
- LIMIT: listings that are suspicious but not clearly fraudulent.
- ALLOW: ordinary buy/sell posts, including high prices.

## 3. Harmful misinformation
- REMOVE: false health or safety claims that could lead someone to physical harm
  (for example, telling people to stop prescribed medicine, or that a dangerous
  practice is safe).
- LIMIT: misleading claims about local events or public services that are not dangerous.
- ALLOW: opinions, predictions, personal experiences, and questions.

## 4. Privacy
- ESCALATE: sharing a private person's home address, phone number, or daily schedule
  together with hostility toward them.
- REMOVE: sharing a private person's contact details, photos of their home, or license
  plates without consent.
- ALLOW: business contact details; a person sharing their own information.

## 5. Impersonation
- REMOVE: accounts or posts pretending to be a specific person, business, government
  agency or Corkboard staff.
- ALLOW: clearly labeled parody.

## 6. Spam and self-promotion
- ALLOW: residents promoting their own local business up to once a week.
- LIMIT: repetitive promotion, or promotion of businesses outside the neighborhood.
- REMOVE: mass-posted identical content, links to unrelated sites, or engagement bait
  ("share this to win").

## When more than one rule applies
Take the most severe action that applies (ESCALATE > REMOVE > LIMIT > ALLOW).
